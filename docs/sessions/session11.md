# Session 11: Roadmap & Risk Planning

## Overview

Session 11 focuses on creating a comprehensive modernization roadmap and risk management plan. This session consolidates all the insights and recommendations from previous sessions to create a structured approach to the modernization journey, with clear phases, milestones, and risk mitigation strategies.

## Pre-Session Activities

### Assessment Team Preparation

- Consolidate risks identified in previous sessions
- Plan potential migration waves and dependencies
- Prepare roadmap visualization templates
- Create risk assessment matrix

### Customer Team Preparation

- Prioritize business units and critical migration paths
- Identify key business milestones and constraints
- Prepare resource availability information
- Review preliminary risk assessment

## Session Activities

### Modernization Roadmap Development

- Define modernization phases and milestones
- Identify dependencies between migration activities
- Create timeline estimates for each phase
- Align roadmap with business priorities and constraints

### Risk Identification and Assessment

- Consolidate risks from all assessment areas
- Categorize risks (technical, operational, business)
- Assess risk probability and impact
- Prioritize risks based on severity

### Mitigation Strategy Development

- Define risk mitigation approaches
- Identify contingency plans for high-priority risks
- Assign risk ownership and monitoring responsibilities
- Create risk tracking mechanisms

## Deliverables

### Migration Roadmap

The Migration Roadmap includes:
- Phased modernization approach
- Key milestones and dependencies
- Resource requirements and timelines
- Success criteria for each phase
- Go/no-go decision points
- Rollback strategies

### Risk Register

The Risk Register documents:
- Comprehensive inventory of identified risks
- Risk categorization and prioritization
- Probability and impact assessment
- Mitigation strategies and contingency plans
- Risk ownership and monitoring approach

### Requirement Traceability Matrix (RTM)

The RTM maps:
- Business requirements to technical solutions
- Requirements to test cases
- Requirements to implementation phases
- Dependencies between requirements

## Modernization Approaches

The session also addresses specific modernization strategy options:

- Strangler Fig Pattern implementation
- Parallel run considerations
- Big Bang vs. Incremental approaches
- Feature flags and canary deployments
- Data migration strategies
- Rollback and recovery plans

## Next Steps

- Prepare for documentation and handover in Session 12
- Refine roadmap based on feedback
- Finalize risk mitigation strategies
- Distribute roadmap and risk documentation for stakeholder review
